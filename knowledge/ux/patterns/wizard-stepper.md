# Wizard / Stepper

## Use
Tasks with meaningful ordered stages, dependencies or cognitive benefit from chunking.

## Avoid
Artificially splitting a short form or forcing sequence where users need flexible navigation.

## Rules
Communicate progress, current step, completed steps and what remains. Define back behavior, persistence, validation gates, optional steps, resume behavior and final review when consequences justify it.

## Edge cases
Refresh, timeout, partial completion, changed upstream answer, invalidated downstream data, abandon/resume and duplicate submission.
